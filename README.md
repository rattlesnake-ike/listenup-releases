# ListenUp

Press the blue circle. Say what you want written. Press it again when you're done.
The words appear in the window and are saved as a Word document in
Documents → ListenUp.

## Install (once)

**[Download ListenUp 1.0.3](https://github.com/rattlesnake-ike/listenup-releases/releases/download/v1.0.3/ListenUp-1.0.3.zip)**

1. Open the downloaded zip. Drag **ListenUp** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open it the first time:
   - Right-click (or Control-click) **ListenUp** → **Open** → **Open**.
   - If your Mac says it "cannot be opened" or "was not opened": go to
     **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**
     next to ListenUp. Then open ListenUp again.
3. Click **OK** when it asks to use the Microphone and Speech Recognition.
4. Turn on Dictation: **System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation**. ListenUp needs it to hear you.

After that, just open ListenUp from Applications. It updates itself.
Needs macOS 10.15 or newer.

## Tips
- Say "new paragraph" to start a new paragraph.
- On Macs older than macOS 13, say the punctuation: "period", "comma", "question mark".
- The two-boxes button at the bottom left copies everything, so you can paste it into an email.
